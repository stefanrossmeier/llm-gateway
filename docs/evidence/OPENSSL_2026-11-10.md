## Candidate evidence

The exact container's `ssl.create_default_context().get_ciphers()` probe ran with
no network, read-only root, UID 10001, no capabilities and no-new-privileges. Its
17 suites are AES-GCM, ChaCha20-Poly1305 and AES-CBC TLS suites; none contains SIV.
The service/owned transport accepts no arbitrary TLS context/cipher/CMS/QUIC
settings from project/request data. Provider endpoints are fixed HTTPS origins.
The proxy permits TCP CONNECT to two exact hosts/443, without TLS interception.
The provider child denies unsolicited DNS/socket access and datagram sends;
network guard and actual two-stack Docker checks cover those restrictions.

Applicable source/test paths: `providers/runtime.py`, `isolation/network.py`,
`service/egress_proxy.py`, `tests/offline/test_network.py`, and
`scripts/check_egress.py`. A transport/provider/cipher configuration change needs
a fresh applicability review. Merely retaining the package version is insufficient.
